Installed: 2011
Last Overhaul: 09-August-2023

------------------------------------------------------------
2. PROBLEM REPORTED
------------------------------------------------------------
Field operator (Shift A) reported abnormal vibration and oil leakage from the
mechanical seal area of P-101A during routine 06:00 hrs round on 14-Mar-2024.
Suction pressure was fluctuating between 3.8 - 4.1 kg/cm2g (normal: 4.5 kg/cm2g).
Discharge temperature recorded at 142 degC (design: 135 degC). Vibration on the
non-drive end (NDE) bearing measured 7.2 mm/s (alarm: 4.5 mm/s; trip: 7.1 mm/s).

------------------------------------------------------------
3. ROOT CAUSE (PRELIMINARY)
------------------------------------------------------------
Inspection by maintenance team indicates worn-out mechanical seal (John Crane
Type 2100) and probable bearing wear on NDE. Fluid contamination suspected due
to degraded seal integrity.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. ACTION PLAN
------------------------------------------------------------
a) Isolate and tag-out P-101A as per SOP-CDU-PUMP-ISOLATION-Rev3.
b) Switch over to standby pump P-101B and monitor.
c) Replace mechanical seal and NDE bearing (SKF 6311 C3).
d) Check shaft alignment per API 686 tolerances.
e) Conduct trial run and record vibration signature.
f) Update equipment history in CMMS after completion.
