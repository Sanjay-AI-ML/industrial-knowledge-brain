BHARAT PETROLEUM CORPORATION LIMITED
Mathura Refinery — Health, Safety and Environment (HSE) Department
INCIDENT REPORT (NEAR-MISS)
============================================================

Incident ID: IR-2024-0156
Date of Incident: 14-March-2024
Time of Incident: 06:15 hrs
Incident Class: Near-Miss (Category 1)
Area / Location: CDU-1 Pump House (Rotary bay)

------------------------------------------------------------
1. DESCRIPTION OF INCIDENT
------------------------------------------------------------
On 14-March-2024 at approximately 06:15 hrs, during Shift A handover rounds, field operator Rakesh Sharma noticed white vapor and oil dripping from the mechanical seal gland of the main Crude Charge Pump P-101A. 
Vibration on the NDE bearing was escalating rapidly to 7.2 mm/s, which exceeded the safety trip limit of 7.1 mm/s. The local alarm sounded on the annunciator panel.

No fire occurred, and there were no injuries. The operator immediately initiated emergency shutdown of P-101A and switched to standby pump P-101B. 
A vapor cloud was prevented by immediately activating the local steam utility to flush the gland area, suppressing volatile hydrocarbons.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. PRELIMINARY ANALYSIS AND ROOT CAUSES
------------------------------------------------------------
The incident was caused by:
- **Mechanical Seal Failure (Machine):** Premature wear on the carbon face of the John Crane 2100 seal.
- **Vibration cyclic load (Machine):** Shaft misalignment (0.12 mm offset) recorded since the last maintenance overhaul in August 2023.
- **Lubricant depletion (Method):** Worn NDE bearings resulting from poor oil circulation, causing bearing heat up to 88 degC.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. IMMEDIATE AND PREVENTATIVE RECOMMENDATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------
- Swap and isolate P-101A as per SOP-CDU-PUMP-ISOLATION-Rev3.
- Inspect and align pump shaft utilizing laser alignment tools per API 686.
- Replace mechanical seal kit and NDE bearing (SKF 6311 C3).
- Review maintenance checklist to ensure alignment checks are signed off by the rotary supervisor.
