PROCEDURE FOR HOT WORK PERMIT AND FIRE SAFETY
IN PROCESS AREAS

------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
------------------------------------------------------------
This procedure defines the minimum safety requirements for carrying out hot
work (welding, cutting, grinding, brazing, and any spark-producing activity)
in all process units, tank farms, and loading gantries of Gujarat Refinery.
It applies to all employees, contractors, and vendor personnel.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. REFERENCES (REGULATORY & CORPORATE)
------------------------------------------------------------
- OISD-117: Standard on Fire Protection Facilities for Petroleum Refineries
  and Petrochemical Plants (latest edition).
- OISD-118: Standard on Layout Requirements for Industrial Installations
  (fire separation distances).
- OISD-105: Work Permit System.
- Petroleum Rules 2002, framed under the Petroleum Act 1934.
- The Factories Act 1948, Chapter IVA (provisions relating to hazardous
  processes), Section 31 to 40.
- PESO (Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organisation) guidelines on storage
  of flammable liquids.
- NFPA 51B: Standard for Fire Prevention During Welding, Cutting, and Other
  Hot Work.
- API RP 2201: Safe Welding, Cutting and Hot Work Practices.

4. PRE-WORK REQUIREMENTS (CONTROLS)
------------------------------------------------------------
a) Conduct Gas Free Test (GFT): LEL must be below 0% (zero) within a 10-metre
   radius of the hot-work spot. Use a calibrated combustible gas detector.
b) Remove all combustible material within an 11-metre (35 ft) radius, OR cover
   with fire-resistant blankets (per OISD-117, clause 6.3).
c) Provide a minimum of two 9-kg Dry Chemical Powder (DCP) fire extinguishers
   and one 50-litre foam trolley at the worksite.
d) Position a fire-watch with a charged fire hose throughout the work and for
   a minimum of 30 minutes after completion.
e) Earth all welding equipment; ensure continuity per IS 3043.
f) Display the hot-work permit at the worksite.
g) Stop all hot work if wind speed exceeds 35 km/h or during a thunderstorm
   warning.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. EQUIPMENT & MONITORING
------------------------------------------------------------
- Combustible gas detector (MSA Altair 5X), calibrated 20-Feb-2024.
- Equipment isolation: E-305 (Kettle reboiler) and V-203 (suction scrubber)
  drained and blinded; blinds recorded in blind register BR-2024-0042.
- Monitor suction scrubber V-203 pressure; must remain below 0.5 kg/cm2g
  during hot work in the vicinity.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. PERMIT VALIDITY & CLOSURE
------------------------------------------------------------
- A hot work permit is valid for a single shift (max 8 hours) from the issue
  time.
- Closure requires sign-off by Performing Authority, Area Authority, and
  Safety Officer, with a final fire-watch confirmation.
- Retain the closed permit for a minimum of 2 years (Factories Act 1948,
  Section 12 records).

------------------------------------------------------------
7. NON-COMPLIANCE
------------------------------------------------------------
Any violation is a reportable safety incident per OISD-105 and shall be logged
in the refinery incident management system (IMS) within 24 hours. Repeat
violations may attract penalties under the Factories Act 1948 and contract
termination.
