KIRLOSKAR BROTHERS LIMITED
ROTARY EQUIPMENT DIVISION — USER MANUAL EXCERPT

DOCUMENT: OEM-MANUAL-KDS-200X150 (Centrifugal Charge Pumps)
Applicable Models: KDS-200x150, KDS-150x100
Section 4: Mechanical Seal and Bearing Assembly

------------------------------------------------------------
1. BEARINGS AND LUBRICATION SPECIFICATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------
- Bearing Type NDE (Non-Drive End): SKF 6311 C3 (Deep groove ball bearing).
- Bearing Type DE (Drive End): SKF 7311 BECBP (Angular contact ball bearings).
- Recommended Lubricant: Mobil DTE Medium or equivalent ISO VG 46 mineral turbine oil.
- Bearing Operating Temperature: Normal range is 55 degC to 75 degC. Maximum allowable bearing temperature before alarm is 85 degC. Mandatory emergency trip at 95 degC.
- Lubricant Replacement Interval: Every 4,000 operating hours or 6 months.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. VIBRATION PARAMETERS AND LIMITS (ISO 10816-3)
------------------------------------------------------------
- Class II Machinery (Medium size pumps, 15 kW to 300 kW, rigid foundation):
  - Zone A (Good): < 1.8 mm/s RMS.
  - Zone B (Satisfactory): 1.8 to 4.5 mm/s RMS. (Alarm threshold set at 4.5 mm/s).
  - Zone C (Unsatisfactory): 4.5 to 7.1 mm/s RMS. (Vibration warning).
  - Zone D (Unacceptable): > 7.1 mm/s RMS. (Mandatory safety trip set at 7.1 mm/s).

------------------------------------------------------------
3. MECHANICAL SEAL LIMITS AND TROUBLESHOOTING
------------------------------------------------------------
- Seal Model: John Crane Type 2100 Elastomer Bellows Seal.
- Maximum Operating Pressure: 15.0 kg/cm2g.
- Maximum Fluid Temperature: 150 degC.
- Recommended Flush Plan: API Plan 11 or API Plan 21 to control heat buildup.
- Troubleshooting Seal Leakage:
  a) Excess vibration (> 4.5 mm/s) leads to bellows distortion, face chipping, and rapid seal leakage.
  b) Worn shaft sleeves or misalignment of shaft (> 0.05 mm parallel offset) induces cyclic loading on the seal faces, leading to premature leakage.
  c) Fluid contamination with particulate matter (> 50 microns) causes abrasive wear on carbon-silicon carbide faces.
