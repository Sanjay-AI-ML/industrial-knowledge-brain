Last Inspection: 12-December-2023

------------------------------------------------------------
2. PROBLEM REPORTED
------------------------------------------------------------
During routine plant inspection, a minor gas leak (bubble test positive, using soapy water) was detected on the main inlet flange gasket of vessel V-203. The flange operating pressure is 12.4 kg/cm2g (design: 18.5 kg/cm2g). Leak detector (Sniffer) registered hydrocarbon concentration of 80 ppm (allowable: < 50 ppm). No structural cracking detected on the vessel shell.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. ROOT CAUSE (PRELIMINARY)
------------------------------------------------------------
- **Thermal Cycling (Environment):** Flange bolts relaxed due to cyclic temperature fluctuations (operating: 110 degC to ambient during unit trips).
- **Gasket degradation (Material):** Spiral wound gasket (316SS with graphite filler) showed signs of corrosion and compression set.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. ACTION PLAN
------------------------------------------------------------
a) Depressurize vessel V-203 as per SOP-CDU-VESSEL-DEPRESSURIZATION.
b) Purge vessel with nitrogen until gas test is zero.
c) Remove flange bolts, clean flange faces.
d) Replace spiral wound gasket (316SS/Graphite, size: 8-inch Class 300).
e) Reinstall and torque bolts in cross-pattern to 120 Nm per BHEL guidelines.
f) Hydrotest flange joint to 15.0 kg/cm2g prior to unit restart.
